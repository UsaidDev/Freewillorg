Physiotherapy for Children and Adults in Tirur

Therapy to improve movement, strength, balance and physical function.

FreeWill Advanced Centre for Mental Health and Rehabilitation offers physiotherapy in Tirur, Malappuram district, as part of a multidisciplinary team that has supported families since 2015.

Who it's for

Children and adults with neurological, developmental, orthopedic or physical difficulties.

Why it helps

To reduce movement difficulties, improve mobility and support physical independence.

How we work

Therapeutic exercises, mobility training, balance activities, strengthening and functional exercises. Every plan starts with an assessment, and progress is reviewed regularly with parents.

Who you'll work with

Physiotherapy at FreeWill is part of a multidisciplinary team of psychologists, therapists and educators who plan your care together. Meet the FreeWill team.

Common questions

Can therapy help my child become more independent?

Yes. Therapy can focus on practical skills such as dressing, feeding, writing, communication, play, learning, mobility and other daily activities according to the child's needs.

Can my child receive more than one type of therapy?

Yes. Some children benefit from a combination of therapies such as Occupational Therapy, Speech Therapy, Physiotherapy, Behavioral Therapy, Remedial Therapy or Special Education.

How often does my child need therapy?

Therapy frequency depends on the child's needs, goals, age, condition and progress. The therapist will recommend an individualized schedule after assessment.

How long will my child's therapy take?

There is no fixed duration. Progress depends on the child's condition, individual needs, consistency of therapy and home support. Goals are reviewed regularly.
