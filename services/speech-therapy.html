Common questions

My child is not speaking yet. Should I be concerned?

Every child develops at their own pace, but delayed speech can sometimes indicate an underlying developmental difficulty. An early assessment can help identify the reason and guide appropriate intervention.

My child understands everything but is not speaking. What should I do?

Good understanding is encouraging, but delayed expressive speech still deserves attention. A speech and developmental assessment can help identify your child's communication needs.

Does my child need Speech Therapy only if they cannot speak?

No. Speech Therapy can also help with language understanding, pronunciation, fluency, social communication, voice and other communication difficulties.

When should I take my child for a developmental assessment?

If you notice delays in speech, movement, learning, attention, social interaction, play or daily activities, it is better to seek an assessment early rather than wait.
