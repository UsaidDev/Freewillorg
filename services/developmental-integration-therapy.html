Who it's for

Children with developmental delays, learning difficulties, sensory challenges or developmental concerns.

Why it helps

To support motor, sensory, cognitive, emotional and functional development.

How we work

Individualized developmental activities designed around the child's abilities and needs. Every plan starts with an assessment, and progress is reviewed regularly with parents.

Who you'll work with

Developmental Integration Therapy at FreeWill is part of a multidisciplinary team of psychologists, therapists and educators who plan your care together. Meet the FreeWill team.

Common questions

When should I take my child for a developmental assessment?

If you notice delays in speech, movement, learning, attention, social interaction, play or daily activities, it is better to seek an assessment early rather than wait.

Does my child need therapy if they have a developmental delay?

Therapy may help your child develop important skills and become more independent. The type and frequency of therapy depend on the child's individual needs.

Is early intervention really important?

Yes. Identifying developmental difficulties early allows appropriate support to begin sooner and gives the child more opportunities to develop important skills.

How can I know which therapy my child needs?

You do not have to decide this yourself. A professional assessment can help identify your child's strengths, difficulties and appropriate therapy needs.
